# Electric Vehicle (EV) Market Analysis

## Project Overview

This project explores Electric Vehicle (EV) market data using Python, Pandas, and Matplotlib. The analysis focuses on understanding market trends, brand performance, pricing strategies, customer satisfaction, vehicle specifications, and value-for-money offerings.

The goal is to identify key factors influencing EV sales and customer preferences while demonstrating practical data analysis and visualization skills.

---

## Dataset Information

The dataset contains information about EV models across multiple manufacturers, including:

* Brand and Model Details
* Pricing Information
* Battery Capacity
* Driving Range
* Charging Speed
* Performance Metrics (Horsepower, Torque, Top Speed, Acceleration)
* Safety Ratings
* Customer Ratings
* Market Segment
* Annual Sales Volume
* Country of Origin

## Key Insights

### Sales Performance

* Tesla emerged as the market leader, followed by BYD and Volkswagen.
* EV sales are concentrated among a small number of major manufacturers.
* Premium and Mid-range segments contribute the highest sales volumes.

### Customer Preferences

* Customer ratings show only a weak relationship with vehicle price.
* Vehicles with higher range generally receive better customer ratings.
* Safety ratings remain consistently high across most brands.

### Technical Findings

* Battery capacity and driving range show an extremely strong positive correlation.
* Higher-priced vehicles typically provide greater horsepower and torque.
* Technical specifications alone do not strongly explain sales performance.

### Value-for-Money Analysis

* Several BYD models achieved the highest value-for-money scores.
* Affordable EVs often provide better value than premium alternatives.

## Conclusion

The analysis shows that while vehicle specifications such as battery capacity and range significantly influence customer perception, sales performance is also strongly affected by factors such as brand reputation, market positioning, and pricing strategy. Premium and Mid-range EVs currently dominate the market, while value-focused manufacturers such as BYD offer some of the most competitive products.
